About

I'm a 47-year-old making a deliberate shift from a career in rope access and construction work into software engineering. I have always been passionate about knowing how things work, and I have found development to be a new avenue where I can continue to evolve and learn. I enjoy solving problems, and seeing things come to life is always a pleasure.

I am passionate about both frontend and backend, but where I am enjoying myself the most is working with chips and IoT. But don't let that distract you, I also love eating good food! IoT, C programming, embedded systems, servers I love tinkering and researching. I don't always manage to let go of things that catch my attention, and I've learned that at certain times the shortest path is straight through, though some obstacles are well worth a good detour.

I am currently working while finishing my degree in Web Application Development (DAW) at ILERNA, and I plan to continue my studies with a degree in Software Engineering at the UOC. Although I currently lack professional experience in the development world, I try to make up for it with a wide range of projects.

If you have read this far, it means I have caught your attention, so please imagine me giving you the Vulcan salute and ...

"live long and prosper!"
